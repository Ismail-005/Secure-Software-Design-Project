Secure Core Banking System

No Slop. No Theater. Real Banking Software.

This direction treats SCBS like internal financial infrastructure, not a generic startup dashboard. Sharp type, warm neutral surfaces, exact numerics, and controls that look built for operators who move money and answer for it.

Design Thesis

The system should feel trustworthy, deliberate, and slightly severe. The kind of product where approvals, locks, fraud review, and audit logs look native, not bolted on.

Internal dashboard Banking operations Anti AI slop Warm neutral palette

Typography

Three fonts. Three jobs. Nothing ornamental, nothing default. Headers do the identity work. Body copy stays readable. Numbers and audit references get a mono voice so the system feels exact.

Body / UI Copy

Source Sans 3 is clean enough for dense dashboards and forms. It does not call attention to itself. Good. That job belongs to the data and the decisions.

Color System

Color is restrained. Most of the UI lives in warm paper neutrals. Accent color is reserved for action, focus, and directional emphasis. Warnings and fraud states read fast without turning the whole product into a Christmas tree.
