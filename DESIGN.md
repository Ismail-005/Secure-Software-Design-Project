# Design System - Secure Core Banking System

## Product Context
- **What this is:** A secure core banking dashboard built for account management, transfers, fraud review, approvals, and audit visibility.
- **Who it's for:** Bank staff operating internal workflows, plus secondary customer-facing authentication and account surfaces.
- **Space/industry:** Banking and fintech operations software.
- **Project type:** Internal dashboard web app.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** Industrial-utilitarian finance.
- **Decoration level:** Minimal with intent.
- **Mood:** Serious, exact, and trustworthy. The product should feel like software used to move money under scrutiny, not a startup template wearing a banking costume.
- **Reference sites:** Mercury, Ramp, Brex, Qonto, Wise Business.

## Typography
- **Display/Hero:** `IBM Plex Sans Condensed` - strong, institutional, and specific without turning theatrical.
- **Body:** `Source Sans 3` - readable at dense dashboard sizes and quiet enough for heavy operational use.
- **UI/Labels:** `Source Sans 3` - consistent form and navigation readability.
- **Data/Tables:** `IBM Plex Mono` - gives balances, ledger rows, transaction IDs, timestamps, and audit references a precise voice.
- **Code:** `IBM Plex Mono`
- **Loading:** Google Fonts for prototypes. Self-host or vendor locally if this moves beyond coursework.
- **Scale:**
  - Display XL: `5.5rem`
  - Display L: `3rem`
  - H1: `2rem`
  - H2: `1.5rem`
  - Body L: `1.125rem`
  - Body: `1rem`
  - Body S: `0.95rem`
  - Meta / mono labels: `0.78rem`

## Color
- **Approach:** Restrained.
- **Primary:** `#0F4C5C` - the main accent for approvals, key actions, active states, and directional emphasis.
- **Secondary:** `#8C6A2B` - reserved for secondary highlights and balance points that need warmth.
- **Neutrals:**
  - Background: `#F3F0E8`
  - Surface: `#FBFAF7`
  - Surface 2: `#F7F4EE`
  - Surface 3: `#F0EBE2`
  - Border: `#D8D1C5`
  - Primary text: `#161A1F`
  - Muted text: `#5F6772`
- **Semantic:**
  - Success: `#1F6B4F`
  - Warning: `#B06A1E`
  - Error: `#B13A3A`
  - Info: `#275D8C`
- **Dark mode:** Keep the same hue relationships, deepen surfaces to charcoal-blue, and reduce accent saturation slightly so the UI stays controlled instead of glowing.

## Spacing
- **Base unit:** `8px`
- **Density:** Compact-comfortable.
- **Scale:** `2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64`

## Layout
- **Approach:** Grid-disciplined shell with editorial hierarchy in page titles and major summary bands.
- **Grid:** 12-column content grid on desktop, collapsing to 6 and then 1 on smaller screens.
- **Max content width:** `1600px`
- **Border radius:** `4px` for controls, `8px` for panels and grouped containers, `9999px` only for status pills.

## Motion
- **Approach:** Minimal-functional.
- **Easing:** `ease-out` for entry, `ease-in` for exit, `ease-in-out` for movement and state changes.
- **Duration:** micro `50-100ms`, short `150-250ms`, medium `250-400ms`

## Safe Choices
- Familiar internal-app shell with left navigation, top actions, and dense content bands.
- Visible approvals, fraud review, and audit surfaces as first-class parts of the product.
- Restrained semantic colors so risk states read immediately.

## Deliberate Risks
- Warm neutral background instead of cold SaaS gray, to avoid cloned fintech sameness.
- Condensed display typography for headers, to give the product an identity that does not feel autogenerated.
- Visible mono treatment for balances, references, and audit data, to make control surfaces feel exact.

## Implementation Notes
- No purple gradients.
- No generic three-column feature-grid marketing patterns inside the app.
- No oversized rounded cards or bubbly UI.
- Avoid using accent color as a blanket fill. Most surfaces should stay neutral.
- Keep tables dense, legible, and aligned. This is banking software. Sloppy spacing ruins the whole thing.
